### 🎥 [Qwen 3.6 35B on 12GB VRAM: Strata vs llama.cpp Tested (Alex / @AlexFP16)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EudtrgdS4JU)
* **Recorded:** 2026-11-14 13:25:20 | **Tags:** `#MoE-Architecture` `#Speculative-Decoding` `#Cache-Hierarchy` `#Memory-Bandwidth` `#Quantization` | **Transcript Length:** 1,760 words
* **Video ID:** `EudtrgdS4JU` | **Author:** Alex (@AlexFP16)

#### Key Findings & Technical Breakdown:
- **Baseline Hardware Tested:** RTX 3060 12GB (GDDR6 360 GB/s) + AMD Ryzen 5 5600 (6 cores) + 64GB DDR4.
- **Model File:** `Unsloth/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_XL` (23 GB GGUF with MTP layer).
- **Core Results & Discrepancies:**
  - Strata claimed ~64 t/s single request decode and 1.5x throughput over `llama.cpp` on an RTX 3060.
  - On Alex's 6-core Ryzen 5600 + 64GB RAM, Strata dropped to **~29 t/s** decode (falling to 23 t/s on 8k prompts), while `llama.cpp` with MoE expert cache (PR #30966) hit **~51 t/s**, and with MTP speculation hit **~67 t/s**.
  - 4-request parallel throughput: `llama.cpp` achieved **85 t/s** vs. Strata's **32 t/s**. Strata won only on prompt evaluation (**1,300 t/s** vs. **1,100 t/s**).
  - In Strata 0.2.12, MTP speculative drafting was completely inactive (0 drafted tokens across 180 requests).
  - Strata parked 4.5 GB base weights on GPU, leaving room for only 3 experts per layer in VRAM, throwing the bulk of the MoE computation onto the 6-core CPU in DDR4.
- **Hardware Contrast with Our Machine (RX 6700 XT):**
  - **Identical CPU & RAM:** Both have AMD Ryzen 5 5600 (6C/12T) + 64GB DDR4.
  - **Divergent Silicon:** RX 6700 XT 12GB brings **~384 GB/s** memory bus (+6.7% vs 360 GB/s) + **96 MB on-die Infinity Cache** (>1 TB/s SRAM) under the Vulkan backend vs. RTX 3060 CUDA (3 MB L2).
- **Test Protocol for Community Report Back:**
  1. Benchmark `Unsloth Qwen 3.6 35B A3B UD-Q4_K_XL` on RX 6700 XT Vulkan across `-ngl 16` (standard offload) vs. `--moe-cache-mib 7680 --moe-cache-cpu-share 1.0` vs. `--spec-type draft-mtp -K 2`.
  2. Measure median decode t/s across 3 interleaved rounds with warmup, tracking MTP draft acceptance $\alpha$.
  3. Compare prompt ingest and decode scaling against Alex's numbers to isolate memory architecture and Vulkan vs CUDA divergence.
